# Website guide for club volunteers

You don't need any technical knowledge to update the website. Everything is done in your web browser.

## Logging in

1. Go to **your-website-address/admin/**.
2. Log in with the email address the club invited. The first time, you'll set a password from the invitation email.

Every change you save goes live on the website within a couple of minutes.

## Fixtures – mostly automatic

Fixtures and results are copied from **FA Full-Time** every morning. You don't need to type them in.

- If something is wrong on our site, check Full-Time first: fixing it there fixes it here the next morning.
- If you must change a fixture by hand (for example a late venue change), edit it under **Club settings → Fixtures** and tick **Locked**, so the daily update doesn't undo your change.

## Writing a news article

1. Click **News → New Article**.
2. Fill in the title, date, category and a one-line summary.
3. Write the article. **Never use children's surnames.**
4. Click **Publish**.

The three starter articles are samples – edit or delete them.

## Adding photos

Use **Club settings → Gallery**. Only upload photos where **every child pictured has photo consent** recorded on their registration. Describe the photo without naming anyone (e.g. "U8 Cubs celebrating after the game").

## Updating club details

**Club settings → Club details** holds contacts, fees, social links and the club timeline. Leave a field empty if it isn't known yet – the website simply hides it.

Most urgent: **Club Welfare Officer**. Until it's filled in, the Safeguarding page tells parents to contact the Club Secretary.

## Adding a new team

1. Find the team on FA Full-Time and open its page. The address contains `teamID=` (and `divisionseason=`). Copy those numbers.
2. In **Club settings → Teams**, add the team with its name exactly as on Full-Time, nickname, age group and the IDs.
3. Its fixtures appear the next morning.

## Who can do what

Everyone invited to the admin area can edit everything. Only invite committee members, and remove people when they leave a role (Netlify → Identity). If you'd like coaches to have access to their own team only, that needs a different setup – ask whoever looks after the site.

## Help

If something looks broken, don't worry – every change is saved in the website's history and can be undone. Contact whoever looks after the site.
